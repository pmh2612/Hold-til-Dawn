Character sprites in this folder
================================
scout, healer, engineer (CHR-01..03), grunt, spitter, wrecker (CHR-05..07):
original 32x32 pixel art (Wrecker 40x40) for Hold 'til Dawn, following the GDD style guide
(palette, dark outline, 8 directions: right, down, left, up and the four 3/4 diagonals (the front or
back silhouette with the face turned), left mirrored; idle 2 frames + walk 4 frames with a 1 px body bounce).
Each frame sits in a cell with 8 px of room around the body (48x48, Wrecker 56x56). After idle and walk:
4 frames per attack (players: bare hands, stone spear, metal blade, hammer, bow; Grunt claw rake,
Spitter spit, Wrecker ground smash), then 4 knock-down frames (hit, knees give, falling, lying with the
eyes shut), all made by tools/PixelPoses.cs; chars.json "anims" lists [first column, frames, fps].

Made by tools/PixelChars.cs: every sprite is written there as a pixel map (one letter per pixel);
the script adds the outline, the ground shadow and the walk/idle frames, and writes these PNGs.
No third-party asset and no AI image generator was used.

AI disclosure: the pixel maps and the script were written by an AI coding assistant (Claude)
from the team's GDD; declare them in GDD Appendix B as AI-made assets. The PNGs are plain
1x pixel art: the team can repaint them in Aseprite / Piskel and the game uses the edited files.

Bosses (boss_*.png, bosses.json)
--------------------------------
juggernaut, broodmother, bio_cannon, burrower, gatebreaker, phantom, ironhide (AGT-05..11):
original 48x48 pixel art, same sheet layout as above (8 direction rows; idle 2 + walk 4,
attack 4: wind-up, slam, impact, recover; knocked down 4).
Each is the NOX brute with the part that shows its move (horns, egg sacs, spore pods, claws,
ram fist, hood and cloak, front plates). Made by tools/PixelBosses.cs from shaded shapes;
same AI disclosure as above.

Harvesting extension: tools/PixelGatherPoses.cs adds 6 frames per action in all 8 directions
for Scout, Healer and Engineer, using their own body, clothes, gear and skin palette.
Columns 30-35: wood axe, 36-41: stone/titanium pickaxe, 42-47: hand-picking herbs.
Original idle, walk, attack and knocked-down cells keep their positions and pixels.
These original code-drawn additions were made with Codex; no third-party images were used.

Enemy readability redesign
-------------------------
Native pixel generator tools/PixelEnemyDesigns.cs supplies separate palettes and silhouettes:
Grunt: rust orange, bone crest and exposed ribs; Spitter: leaf green, long neck and lime acid sacs;
Wrecker: slate blue, squared stone shoulders and a cracked chest plate.
All eight facings include idle, walking, attack and fallen frames.
Bosses: rust/bone Juggernaut; coral/cream Broodmother with six insect legs;
forest/lime Bio-Cannon with spore chimneys; ochre/ivory Burrower with back ridges;
bronze/verdigris Gatebreaker with a square ram; ice/cyan Phantom with pointed hood and ribbons;
blue/steel Ironhide with angular armour fins. Bosses are displayed at 3x instead of 2x
(1.5 times their previous visual size), including attacks and corpses.
Review: tools/EnemyDesignPreview.tscn (headless: validates 1,120 sprite cells;
graphical: exercises eight facings, attacks and corpses and saves review screenshots under .tmp).

Integrated player combat sprites
--------------------------------
art/chars/scout.png, healer.png and engineer.png; chars.json per-role combat regions:
9 attack variants x 8 directions x 6 complete poses for each role (1,296 frames).
Body, role-coloured sleeves/skin, hands, weapon and depth ordering are authored together
by tools/player_combat_art.gd using the existing PixelChars body/palette helpers.
Frames are tightly packed with transparent gutters and individual foot anchors.
These code-authored additions were made with Codex; no external or AI-generated images.
The original walking, gathering, downed and working cells are preserved in the top band.
All complete combat sprites are packed below that band in the same role PNG.
There are no separate weapon-effect PNGs; runtime and AnimViewer use the same regions.
Enemy sheets are preserved.

Held-item player sprites
------------------------
Each role PNG also contains two idle and four walking poses in eight directions
for all 25 inventory items, three radio components and empty hands (4,176 poses).
Role bodies, hands and carried items are baked into each complete frame offline
by tools/player_combat_art.gd; ordinary item art reuses the project item PNGs.
Keys and radio components use matching native pixel shapes and palettes.
Identical poses share atlas regions; no runtime weapon/item composition is used.
chars.json per-role held clips index these poses. Original top-band cells remain intact.

Held weapons are rasterized at half their former size around the grip, before
full-size hands/body are drawn. Resting weapons point downward beside the body.
Attack sprites use the same compact weapon dimensions; a shared runtime ground
stroke shows damage reach. Harvesting art is unchanged. Nearest-neighbour
authoring preserves hard pixel edges.

Backpack icon: art/items/backpack.png is a native 16px pixel icon authored
by tools/bag_art.gd, using the existing item-art style. Backpack
carrying poses are included in each player sheet for item-art completeness.

Juggernaut skill sprites
-----------------------
The same boss_juggernaut.png includes 144 additional full-body poses in eight
directions: slam_wind (6), slam_hit (4), charge_wind (4), charge_run (4).
Authored offline by tools/pixel_enemies.gd --only=juggernaut. The original
14 columns, six other boss sheets and three regular enemies are preserved.
Runtime selects these atlas clips from the authoritative special timing.

Player hit-reaction sprites
---------------------------
Each role PNG includes hit_launch and hit_knockback: six complete character
poses, eight directions, 288 poses across Scout, Healer and Engineer.
Authored offline by tools/player_combat_art.gd using the original role body
and palette, with flailing arms, bent knees and a baked backward tilt.
chars.json per-role reactions index the packed poses. Runtime adds the
vertical rise/fall above a ground shadow; existing combat, held-item and
walking/harvesting pixels and their atlas coordinates are preserved.

Juggernaut slam timing: slam_wind plays its six frames over 1.5 seconds;
slam_hit holds its four impact/stand-up frames for 0.65 seconds.
The simulation and renderer share these timings; no additional PNG is loaded.

NOX-02 Chainwarden replaces the old Broodmother design. Its legacy boss_broodmother PNG key is retained for compatibility. Iron mask, shoulder chains, chain flail, sweep and hook/pull poses are authored in tools/pixel_enemies.gd (38 columns, eight facings). Rebuild only this boss with tools/PixelEnemies.tscn -- --only=broodmother.
