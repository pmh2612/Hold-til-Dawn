Character sprites in this folder
================================
scout, healer, engineer (CHR-01..03), grunt, spitter, wrecker (CHR-05..07):
original 32x32 pixel art (Wrecker 40x40) for Hold 'til Dawn, following the GDD style guide
(palette, dark outline, 4 directions with left mirrored, idle 2 frames + walk 4 frames).

Made by tools/pixel_chars.gd: every sprite is written there as a pixel map (one letter per pixel);
the script adds the outline, the ground shadow and the walk/idle frames, and writes these PNGs.
No third-party asset and no AI image generator was used.

AI disclosure: the pixel maps and the script were written by an AI coding assistant (Claude)
from the team's GDD; declare them in GDD Appendix B as AI-made assets. The PNGs are plain
1x pixel art: the team can repaint them in Aseprite / Piskel and the game uses the edited files.
